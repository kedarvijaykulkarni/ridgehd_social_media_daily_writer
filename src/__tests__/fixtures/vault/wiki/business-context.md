# business-context.md

## 1. What the product is

An AI-native operations platform for dive centers.

## 2. What you can honestly claim today

**✅ Solid and verifiable — sell on this:**
- Core scheduling, bookings, staff, catalog, POS, partners/commissions, accommodation, gear rental — real, tested business logic
- Order immutability with credit-note-on-change
- RLS multi-tenancy enforced at the database level
- AI copilot for **read and scheduling operations** — genuinely demoable
- **Waiver system** — per-participant enforcement, typed e-signature, RLS-protected. Built-in vs. the incumbent's metered add-on — sell on that contrast.

**❌ Not yet sellable — do not claim present-tense:**

| Gap | Commercial consequence |
|---|---|
| **Money-moving AI actions (refund, create/cancel booking) have no undo** — by design | Do not market "safe, reversible AI" as a blanket claim. |
| **Waiver system lacks QR-linked signing and participant check-in state** | Sell the real waiver feature without these specifics. Roadmap only. |

> ⚠️ **Re-verification pass.** Re-check against current QA status before quoting.

## 3. Market sizing

~10,100 affiliated centers.
